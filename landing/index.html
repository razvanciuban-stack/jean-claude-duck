Your AI-powered desktop companion 🐤

A pixel-art rubber duck that lives on your dock, chats with AI, reacts to what you do, and quacks when you get notifications.

Download for Mac Free & Open Source

> Features

Everything a duck needs to be your best desk buddy.

🧠

AI Brain

Chat with your duck using a local AI (Ollama). No cloud, no API key, 100% private.

👀

Eye Tracking

Jean Claude follows your cursor with his eyes. He's always watching.

💬

Activity Reactions

Coding? Browsing? Slacking? Your duck knows and reacts with little speech bubbles.

🔔

Quack Notifications

Get a satisfying quack sound every time you receive a macOS notification.

📚

Obsidian Brain

Connect your Obsidian vault. Your duck remembers your decisions, lessons, and patterns.

Animations

Roundhouse kicks, splits, bounces — Jean Claude has moves.
